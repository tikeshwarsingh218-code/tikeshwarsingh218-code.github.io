# J. W. Mason

J. W. Mason is an economist whose research focuses on macroeconomics, finance, economic history, and the distribution of economic power. He is an Associate Professor of Economics at John Jay College, City University of New York.

His work examines questions about investment, financial institutions, corporate decision-making, and the way economic power shapes macroeconomic outcomes. He has also written extensively for a wider public audience on economics and economic policy.

Mason is the co-author, with Arjun Jayadev, of *Against Money*. Their work examines the role of money, finance, and economic institutions and questions some conventional ways of thinking about financial markets and economic policy.

His writing combines economic theory with historical and institutional analysis, with an emphasis on understanding how economic systems actually operate rather than treating markets as separate from political and institutional structures.
