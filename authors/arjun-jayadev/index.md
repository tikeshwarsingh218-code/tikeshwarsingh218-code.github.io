# Arjun Jayadev

Arjun Jayadev is a Professor of Economics at Azim Premji University in Bengaluru and Director of the Centre for the Study of the Indian Economy. He is also a Senior Economist at the Institute for New Economic Thinking.

He previously taught Economics at the University of Massachusetts, Boston and was an inaugural post-doctoral fellow at Columbia University's Committee on Global Thought. He holds a PhD in Economics from the University of Massachusetts, Amherst.

His research interests include international economics, economics of distribution, political economy, macroeconomic dynamics, and the economics of power. His research combines quantitative and theoretical approaches to questions involving finance, development, political economy, and intellectual property.

Jayadev has written extensively on income and wealth distribution, financial systems, labour, macroeconomic policy, inequality, and the distributional consequences of financial institutions. He has also worked on questions involving household debt, financial reform, interest rates, and macroeconomic policy.

He has collaborated extensively with J. W. Mason on research concerning household debt, financial systems, macroeconomic policy, and distribution. He is the co-author of *Against Money*.
