# M. R. Sharan

M. R. Sharan is an economist and researcher whose work focuses on development economics, political economy, governance, and public policy in India. His research examines how state institutions, public programmes, and local political structures shape the lives and opportunities of people, particularly in rural areas.

His work has included research on bureaucracy, governance, poverty alleviation, and Panchayati Raj institutions. He has also worked extensively with field-based data and research methods to understand how public institutions function on the ground.

Sharan is the author of *Last Among Equals: Power, Caste and Politics in Bihar's Villages*, published in 2021. The book examines village politics in Bihar through the interaction of caste, power, local institutions, and political change.

His research combines economic analysis with detailed field research, with particular attention to the relationship between formal institutions and the social and political structures within which they operate.
